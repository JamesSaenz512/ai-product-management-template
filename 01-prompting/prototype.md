https://juno-pm-dashboard--jbsaenz512.replit.app/

## What it demonstrates

It takes raw product input, pulls out the most useful insights, and summarizes the opportunities. I also adjusted the prompt to narrow the results from three insights down to the two that mattered most.

## Debrief

- **What worked:** It did what I expected it to do. The output was clear and the three-column layout made it easy to see the original input, the insights, and the summary.
- **What broke / felt like a toy:** Right now the input is manual and it isn’t connected to real RocketShip data. It also doesn’t show where the insights came from, so I’d still want to validate the results before acting on them.
- **What I'd change next pass:** I’d connect it to actual data from Slack, Jira, and Notion and show the source behind each insight. I’d also add a review step so a PM has to approve anything before it affects priorities or delivery decisions.
