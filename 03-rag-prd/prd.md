## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need evidence-based prioritisation. Juno reads the strategy and the customer signal, ranks the backlog, and defends every call with a citation, inside limits a human set on purpose.

## 01 Context · Data Requirements

**Required sources, and exclusions**

Required sources: Juno can retrieve from Slack #escalations threads, Jira P0/P1 issues and their metadata from the last 90 days, support tickets from the last 90 days, and the current RocketShip Strategy One-Pager stored in Notion. These sources provide the customer signal, delivery history, and company priorities needed to synthesize and prioritize risks.

Excluded: Juno does not use private Slack DMs, HR/employee records, customer contracts, unrelated financial data, or tickets older than 90 days unless a PM explicitly requests historical research. It also excludes superseded strategy documents so outdated priorities do not influence recommendations.

**SLA of truth**

Changes should show up in Juno within about 15 minutes. If the data is more than 24 hours old, Juno should clearly call that out. If a required source is unavailable, Juno should say which source it can’t reach and mark the answer as incomplete or unverified rather than guessing.

## 02 Tools · System Capabilities

**The verb list**

Read — Search the current RocketShip Strategy 
Read — Read the relevant Slack threads.
Read — Pull related support tickets from the last 90 days.
Read — Pull related Jira P0/P1 bugs and incidents 
Draft — Create a proposed priority ranking and include justification
Write — Update the roadmap only after explicit PM confirmation.

Send: Nothing generated or output independently of PM

**Deliberate omissions**

Juno cannot send emails, post to Slack, or communicate directly with customers. Those actions stay with a PM until we’ve built enough trust in the system.

## 03 Loop · AI Costs & Latency

**Turn ceiling and escalation**

Juno gets a maximum of 5 turns per request or response time exceeds 30 seconds.

**Latency and cost target**

If a task exceeds 30 seconds, Juno stops and hands it back to a human rather than continuing to burn time and cost. Cost per task should stay under $0.12.

## 04 Memory · Data Requirements

**What persists, at what scope**

Juno remembers ranking reasons for the sprint and user corrections for 90 days. Nothing should automatically carry over between teams.

**Expiry and write rules**

Each stored fact gets an expiration date. User corrections are kept for 90 days and always override Juno’s older stored answer. If they conflict, the human correction wins.

## 05 Permissions · AI Risks & Mitigations

**The autonomy dial**

| Side-effect class | Tier |
|---|---|
| read | auto |
| draft | auto |
| write | confirm |
| send | blocked |

**Justification**

Read — Auto: Very low blast radius. It only looks at information and does not change anything. Nothing to undo.
Draft — Auto: Low blast radius. A bad draft is only a suggestion and can be edited or deleted before anyone acts on it.
Write — Confirm: Medium blast radius. A bad roadmap update could affect the whole team and influence decisions. It can be undone, but someone may already have acted on the bad information.
Send — Blocked: High blast radius. Customer-facing communication leaves the company and could affect customers or RocketShip’s reputation. It may be impossible to fully undo once sent.

## 06 Verification · AI Testing & Measurement

**The check before output ships**

Before Juno shows a recommendation, it checks that the recommendation is supported by the RocketShip strategy. If it can point to a matching strategy clause, it passes. If it cannot, Juno marks the recommendation as unverified.

**Failure behaviour**

If the check fails, Juno still shows the recommendation, but clearly marks it as unverified and explains what is missing. It should not pretend the answer is correct, and it should not hide the result.
