## Goal

Reduce risk by finding and prioritizing important P0 escalations so PMs can respond faster.

**Primary actor:** Human + Agent co-pilot

## Trigger

A new P0 thread appears and reaches 5 or more messages within 10 minutes.

## Steps & tools

**Pattern:** ReAct (single-agent reason-act-observe loop)

| Step | Action | Tool / model | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read the Slack escalation thread. | Slack thread: Read-only | - Juno can read Slack, Jira, support tickets, and strategy information on its own.  - Juno can prepare a Jira draft, but the PM has to approve it before anything is written.  - Juno cannot send customer messages, post to Slack on its own, or make roadmap changes. |
| 2 | Check Jira, support tickets, and current strategy for anything related. | Jira search: Read-only |  |
| 3 | Compare what it finds and figure out how serious the risk is. | Support Tickets: Read-only |  |
| 4 | Put together a short recommendation with the reason and supporting evidence. | Strategy Documents: Read-only |  |
| 5 | Send it to the PM to review. | Jira Suggestion: Draft-only |  |

**Schemas**

- Slack returns the thread messages and basic thread details.
- Jira returns matching issues, status, priority, and links.
- Support returns related tickets and customer impact.
- Strategy returns the relevant company priorities and supporting text.
- Jira draft returns a proposed issue for the PM to review.

**Memory (in or out of scope)**

- **Episodic:** Keep track of what Juno does during this escalation, including the searches it runs and what it finds. Clear it when the task is done.
- **Semantic:** Keep Juno’s basic rules and the PM’s preferred way of seeing risk information. Do not permanently store customer-specific issue details or contracts.
- **Working:** Keep the Slack thread, related Jira issues, support tickets, strategy info, and anything else Juno is actively using to work the issue.
- **External:** Use Slack for the P0 issue, Jira for related issues, support tickets for customer impact, and the strategy knowledge base for company priorities. These should be the source of truth, not Juno’s memory.

## Human-in-the-loop

The PM takes over if Juno is missing required information, confidence is below 70%, or before Juno makes any Jira or roadmap change.

## Success & failure

- **Done when:** - Success: Juno has enough information to make a recommendation with at least 70% confidence.
- Failure: Juno can’t get to a source it needs or a tool keeps failing.
- Escalation: Juno isn’t confident enough, the information doesn’t line up, or the decision needs PM judgment.
- Timeout: If it takes more than 30 seconds, stop and hand it back to the PM.
- **Fails safe when:** - Juno can read Slack, Jira, support tickets, and strategy information on its own. 
- Juno can prepare a Jira draft, but the PM has to approve it before anything is written. 
- Juno cannot send customer messages, post to Slack on its own, or make roadmap changes.
